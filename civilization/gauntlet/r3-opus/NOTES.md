# Ashes & Seeds (civilization, round 3, opus)

An idle civilization in detailed isometric pixel art: a new people settles on the overgrown ruins of a real city and grows from thatched huts to brick and steam, and then into one of two futures, solar glass gardens or humming crystal spires.

## How to play
Leave it running. The town grows by itself every few seconds, roads wear from dirt to cobbles to stone, old houses are rebuilt in each new age's style, and the camera drifts between the whole town and a closer street view, following the news. Sheep and cows graze the fields, deer and wolves keep to the woods, dogs and cats roam the streets, boats sail the river, and carts, trams or pods carry people about as the eras change. Light follows the real sun at the chosen place, so at night the windows and the road lamps glow.

The first tap starts the music and shows a short hint. After that, tap a person to read their name, age and story and whisper one idea to them, once in their life: Invent pushes the town towards the future and the solar branch, Believe gathers a festival and leans towards the crystal branch, and Rebel brings a golden age of growth. Tap a place to suggest a road, a farm, a market or a temple there; this works once every 30 minutes and they listen only about 70% of the time. The two corner buttons, which show for a few seconds after a tap, pick a new place on Earth (this starts a new world) and mute the sound (also the M key).

Events come on their own every minute or so: festivals, weddings, fires (with flames, then rebuilding), storms with rain and lightning, hard winters with snow, plague, traders, monsters from the old tunnels in the fantasy age and strange signals in the future. The first one, a festival, shows after about 25 seconds.

URL options for testing: `?hour=HH` forces the time of day, `?weather=rain|snow` the weather, `?fast=1` runs ten times faster and does not save, and `?seed=N&place=paris` opens a read-only world from that seed (the "friend's seed" window).

## What I reused
The Clockscapes music generator (`<script id="lofi">`, copied whole) plays the music, with a style per era and culture: the seed picks a culture (Celtic, Galician, Andean, steppe, Slavic or Persian scales and instruments) for the first age, Victorian or Balkan brass for the industrial age, and techno or dungeon synth for the solar or magic future. Darker moods come with fires, plague and monsters, and night follows the real sun. `sunPos` is copied from Clockscapes' world code.

## Art
All art is generated by two scripts in `tools/` (plain Node, no dependencies, they write their own PNGs): `make-ground.mjs` paints the terrain tiles and the beings, and `make-buildings.mjs` (made by an Opus art helper) paints the ruins, the buildings of every era with their night-window frames, and the trees, bushes and rocks. The page draws only from `art/index.json`, and a missing frame becomes a plain placeholder with one warning.

## What is rough or left out
The terrain is a rough sketch of each city (which side the sea is on, whether a river crosses it), not real map data. Weather is from events, not Open-Meteo. People walk randomly along the roads rather than going to work and home, and the chronicle is kept in the save but there is no page to read it yet. The future eras get tall quickly in a crowded town, and the windmill and station sprites are the weakest. There are no tests yet and no `docs/`.
